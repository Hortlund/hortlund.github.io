Questions

Before your first set

The useful details, without making you dig through a support manual.

Is We Go Jim free?

Yes. We Go Jim is free to download from the App Store.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. Core workouts, templates, history, exercises, active workout drafts, and profile features are designed to remain usable on your device.

What can I track?

You can track sets, reps, weight, supersets, drop stages, cardio duration, distance and pace, rest timers, notes, personal records, training volume, templates, goals, and progress trends. Outdoor walks, runs, and bike rides can record GPS routes with permission. Saved history powers training journeys, achievement goals, shareable milestones and personal records, and yearly recaps.

Does We Go Jim connect to Apple Health?

Yes, if you enable it. Apple Health export is off by default. With permission, it saves newly completed workouts; estimated active calories have a separate opt-in. WGJ does not request access to other apps' Health data. Later edits or deletions in WGJ do not change records already saved to Health.

Do I need to allow location access?

Only to record GPS routes for Outdoor Walk, Outdoor Run, or Outdoor Bike. With Precise Location permission, recording can continue while your screen is locked; pausing or finishing stops GPS. You can log other workouts without location access and enter cardio distance manually. Completed routes can join your private iCloud backup.

How do I request a feature or report a bug?

Message Andreas directly on X. Screenshots, steps to reproduce, and what you expected to happen are especially useful.

What the app does

We Go Jim helps you log strength and cardio workouts, organize templates with supersets and drop stages, review workout history, track training journeys and yearly recaps, import or export workout templates, manage profile widgets, and back up supported data to iCloud when available. Optional features include outdoor GPS recording, calorie estimates, Apple Health export, and workout Live Activities. Core workout features are local-first. Cloud backup, restore, and backup comparison features require iCloud and CloudKit availability.

Workout safety

Train at your own risk. Stop exercising and seek qualified medical help if you feel pain, dizziness, shortness of breath, or anything unsafe. Talk to a doctor or qualified professional before training if you have an injury, medical condition, medication concern, or health question.

They are generated from available app data and may be incomplete, delayed, incorrect, or unsuitable for your situation. Calorie estimates and outdoor recording

Calorie estimates are approximate calculations from the details you enter and your logged activity, not sensor measurements or medical advice. GPS routes, distance, and pace depend on location permission, signal quality, and device behavior and may contain gaps or errors. Outdoor recording is a workout log, not a navigation or emergency service. Use your judgment about your surroundings and route.

Apple Health and Live Activities

Apple Health export is optional and requires Apple's permissions. When enabled, it saves newly completed workouts and, with a separate opt-in, available estimated active calories. It does not automatically export older workout history or keep Apple Health records synchronized with later edits or deletions in We Go Jim. Turning export off or using Delete My Data does not remove records already saved to Apple Health; manage those in the Health app.

Live Activities are optional system displays of workout progress. Their availability and updates depend on iOS settings and device state. Workout details may be visible on the Lock Screen. Manage permissions and review the Privacy Policy for storage, backup, sharing, and deletion behavior.
